# Sample tracks

Drop two royalty-free tracks (mp3/wav/m4a) in this directory. The app lists them
under "Use sample track" and treats each filename as its song_id.

Suggested royalty-free sources:
- https://pixabay.com/music/
- https://www.chosic.com/free-music/all/
- https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/

The two files are intentionally not committed. Only upload songs you own or that
are royalty free.
